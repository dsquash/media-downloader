Select clips on the timeline, open a tool, and run it. Everything works on the selection only.

✂Rough Cutremove silences

Cuts the pauses out of the selected clips. With stacked clips (two mics, multicam) a moment goes only when it's silent in all of them. The original sequence is kept as a copy.

🔊Normalizeeven out loudness

Measures each selected clip (EBU R128) and sets its Volume so it lands on the target, keeping peaks under −1 dB. Nothing is rendered — it's just the clip's volume.

🔍Punch-inalternate wide / close

Zooms in on every second selected clip, so jump cuts alternate wide and close instead of looking like skips. Run it again and nothing changes — it never zooms further.

🎥Dynamic Zoomslow animated push-in

A slow zoom across each selected clip, like DaVinci's Dynamic Zoom — two Scale keyframes, first and last frame. Running it again replaces the previous animation.

💥SFX at cutsa sound on every join

Puts your sound on every cut between the selected clips — the joins Rough Cut leaves, or your own. Each lands on the first free audio track; nothing on the timeline moves.

📸 Paste screenshot

Saves the image on your clipboard into the assets folder and imports it. Take a screenshot to clipboard first: Cmd+Ctrl+Shift+4 (Mac) / Win+Shift+S (Windows).
